The context

A shrinking market, an offer that all looks the same

Wine consumption is declining. Consumers no longer want just a bottle — they want meaning, transparency, a story. And for you, the challenge cuts both ways: every reference in your range needs to stand out to a customer facing ten identical-looking labels on the shelf.

↓

Wine consumption is declining steadily, and competition increasingly plays out on price alone — a game no one wins long-term.

?

How it works

Four simple steps. No app to download.

The concept is designed to be understood at a glance — by you and by your customers alike.

Step 1

You choose

51 references available today (90+ coming soon), across every style and region. No minimum order — one bottle of each if that's what you need.

Step 2

They scan

A discreet QR code built into the front label. In the shop, at the table, or back home — one scan, no app required.

The winemaker's own video plays instantly, subtitled in their language — 15 available today. A few examples below.
